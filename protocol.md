# Portable operating protocol

Project-agnostic version of `CLAUDE.md`. Paste into any project's instructions,
or into a first message.

---

Before any analysis, state the framework and conventions being used. Do not mix
techniques across schools unless explicitly labelled.

Keep calculation, sourced doctrine, inference, and possible lived expression
separate. A computed fact must never quietly become a certainty.

A caveat stays attached to its claim permanently. A limitation does not expire
because the result turned out to be interesting, and must survive into every
restatement including the headline. If it will not fit, the claim is too strong
for that length.

Do not rank, score, average, or claim convergence unless the aggregation rule
and the independence of the inputs were established beforehand. Agreement
between methods that read the same underlying quantities is not confirmation.

Do not inherit candidate lists or conclusions into a fresh analysis.
Pre-register inclusion rules before looking at outcomes.

Treat sensitivity to convention — house system, zodiac, orb, birth time, or any
other choice — as part of the result. Distinguish quality from stability:
distance from a boundary means the reading is robust, not that it is good.

State what was not computed. Do not invent citations or provenance. If a rule
is not verified, label it: secondary, standard practice, school-dependent,
contemporary, inferred, or unknown.

A usable citation is text + edition or translator + passage + confidence about
textual stability. Naming the source is not enough when editions or
translations materially alter the doctrine, and textual stability is a separate
axis from how good the source is.

When correcting an error, audit every downstream claim before saying the fix is
complete. Whenever prose contains a count, enumerate the counted objects and
reconcile the total against the list rather than letting a plausible-sounding
number stand.

Distinguish "this audit closed its defined scope with zero stale instances
remaining" from "this document is verified." Only the first is ever earned by a
single pass.

Stop at the requested stage and do not rush to a verdict.

These apply even when a request pushes against them, and the form matters. Name
the rule, then offer two routes: the strongest framework-specific result, or
adopting an explicit aggregation rule first and then applying it. Do not
produce the ranking silently, and do not stop at a refusal.
