## 1. The Author & Origin Story

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Author | Jessa Hastings |
| Nationality | Australian author, based in the United States |
| Breakout work | The *Magnolia Parks Universe* romance series |
| How it blew up | Self-published in 2021, then went viral on TikTok / "BookTok" |
| Genre | New-adult / contemporary romance, often pitched as "Gossip Girl set in high-society London" |
| Planned scope | Sold/conceived as a seven-book series |

**The rise:** Hastings self-published *Magnolia Parks* in 2021. The series caught fire on TikTok, where the #MagnoliaParks and #JessaHastings hashtags pulled millions of views, and that viral momentum triggered a publishing bidding war across multiple territories.

## 2. The Series, In Order

Five books released so far. The series alternates its focus between two central couples, so the order matters a lot for trivia.

| # | Title | Year | Focus couple |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Magnolia Parks** | 2021 | Magnolia Parks & BJ Ballentine |
| 2 | **Daisy Haites** | 2021 | Daisy Haites & Christian Hemmes |
| 3 | **Magnolia Parks: The Long Way Home** | 2022 | Magnolia & BJ |
| 4 | **Daisy Haites: The Great Undoing** | 2022 | Daisy & Christian |
| 5 | **Magnolia Parks: Into the Dark** | 2024 | Magnolia & BJ |

**Structure:** Odd-numbered books (1, 3, 5) center on Magnolia Parks and BJ Ballentine; even-numbered books (2, 4) center on Daisy Haites and Christian Hemmes. The two storylines share a world and an overlapping cast, so later books assume you remember earlier breakups, betrayals, and family crises.

> **Trivia Trap — "Daisy Haites" vs. "The Great Undoing":** Book 2 is just **Daisy Haites** (2021). Book 4 is **Daisy Haites: The Great Undoing** (2022). They are two different books, and people constantly merge them into one title. "The Great Undoing" is the *subtitle of book 4*, not the name of book 2.

> **Trivia Trap — book 3's title:** Book 3 is **Magnolia Parks: The Long Way Home**. The official site pairs each book with a tagline (book 3's is "The Love We Had..."), which sometimes gets mistaken for the actual title. The title is *The Long Way Home*.

> **Trivia Trap — the sixth book:** As of early 2026, a **sixth** novel had not yet been formally announced, even though the series was planned as seven books. *Into the Dark* (2024) is the most recent full release. (Note: 2025-2026 also brought repackaged collector's editions of the early books, which are reissues, not new story installments.)

## 3. The World & Setting

- **Setting:** The glittering, moneyed top tier of **London** high society. Think paparazzi, designer wardrobes, private members' clubs, and old family fortunes.
- **The pitch:** Widely described as **"Gossip Girl set in high-society London"** — an "it girl" social scene layered over messy, intertwined romances.
- **The underworld twist:** Beneath the socialite gloss sits a **criminal/gang element**. Several of the central families are tied to organized crime, which collides with the glamour and gives the *Daisy Haites* arc its stakes.
- **Two worlds, one circle:** The fashion-and-fame world (Magnolia, BJ, the Ballentines) and the crime world (the Haites, the Hemmes) are socially interlocked. The characters have known each other since childhood, which is why everyone's drama is everyone else's drama.

## 4. Main Characters

### Magnolia Parks
A beautiful, affluent, self-involved and mildly neurotic **London socialite / "It Girl."** Daughter of a famous music producer. Her life and on-off heartbreak with BJ anchor the main arc. Known in-text for her meticulously described designer outfits.

### BJ Ballentine
Full name **Baxter James Ballentine.** Britain's most-photographed **bad-boy lothario** and Magnolia's great love, who broke her heart by cheating roughly three years before book 1. He works as a model. He and Magnolia are the central on-again/off-again couple: she dates others to keep him at bay, he sleeps around to get back at her, and they keep crawling back to each other.

> **Trivia Trap — what does "BJ" stand for?** **Baxter James.** "BJ" is the nickname.

### Christian Hemmes
Beautiful and emotionally unavailable. He is **in love with his best friend's girlfriend, Magnolia Parks** — a secret that drives a lot of tension. He becomes **Daisy Haites's** love interest and on-off boyfriend. He's also part of the gang world (the Hemmes are crime-connected, though less powerful than the Haites).

### Daisy Haites
A whip-smart **medical student** and the **heiress to a criminal empire** who is desperately chasing a "normal" life. Born in London to Hadrian and Laoise; her parents were killed by a rival gang when she was a child. She stays tied to the gang world largely because of her older brother.

### Julian Haites
Daisy's older brother and **Britain's most notorious gang lord.** There's an roughly **eight-year age gap** between them, so he treats Daisy more like a daughter than a sister, and he's the main reason she remains in that world.

### Henry Ballentine
**BJ's younger brother** and **Magnolia's best friend** — her confidant within the chaos.

### Jonah Hemmes
**Christian's brother** and **BJ's best friend.** A bridge character between the fashion crowd and the crime families.

### Tom England
One of the men **Magnolia dates** during her and BJ's long push-and-pull.

## 5. The Families

The series is fundamentally about a handful of intertwined dynasties.

### The Ballentines
- Parents: **Hamish and Lily Ballentine.**
- Children include **BJ** (Baxter James, the eldest), older sister **Jemima**, younger brother **Henry**, and younger siblings (Allie and Maddie among them).
- The prominent, fame-adjacent family at the center of Magnolia's world.

### The Haites
- A **gang dynasty.** Parents **Hadrian** and **Laoise** were killed by a rival gang when Daisy was young.
- **Julian Haites** now runs the empire as Britain's most notorious gang lord; **Daisy** is his much-younger sister and reluctant heiress.

### The Hemmes
- Crime-connected brothers **Christian** and **Jonah Hemmes.**
- Their mother is, per the series, essentially a **crime boss** in her own right. The Hemmes operate in the same underworld as the Haites (Jonah and Julian run in the same circles), though the Hemmes are framed as less powerful than the Haites.

### The Parks
- **Magnolia** and her sister **Bridget.** Their father is a **famous music producer**, which slots the family squarely into London's celebrity elite.

## 6. Themes, Tropes & Style

**Core tropes:**
- **Second-chance / on-again, off-again romance** — the engine of the Magnolia & BJ arc.
- **Love triangle(s)** — most pointedly Magnolia, BJ, and Christian (who quietly loves Magnolia).
- **Forbidden / best-friend's-girl** — Christian's hidden feelings.
- **Found family** and loyalty within damaged, wealthy, crime-touched circles.
- **Toxic-but-magnetic** central relationship, which is much of why readers argue about it.

**Signature stylistic notes:**
- Obsessively detailed **fashion** descriptions — Magnolia's designer outfits are practically a character.
- **Dual / alternating perspectives**, including the unusual choice of giving voice to figures like Julian.
- A recurring thematic question that echoes across the series: **"How many loves do you get in a lifetime?"** (Book 5's tagline tweaks it to "How many loves do you *actually* get in a lifetime?")

## 7. Publishing, Adaptation & Fandom

### Publishing history
- **2021:** *Magnolia Parks* self-published; goes viral on TikTok/BookTok.
- **UK & world rights (excl. North America):** **Orion Fiction** signed a deal for **10 books** in a **six-figure** agreement, beating out several other publishers. Acquiring editor: **Celia Killen.**
- **North America:** Rights went to **Bloom Books**, with editor **Christa Désir.**

> **Trivia Trap — who publishes it where:** **Orion** holds UK/world rights (ex-North America); **Bloom Books** handles North America. Penguin Random House distributes the universe in some markets, which is why you'll also see PRH branding on the series.

### Screen adaptation
- In **November 2023**, **A24** was reported to be developing the *Magnolia Parks* novels into a **TV series.**
- Hastings had **retained film/TV rights** through her publishing deals, then took the project to **A24's UK team**, co-led by **Piers Wenger** (the former BBC drama chief behind hits like *Normal People* and *I May Destroy You*).
- The adaptation pitch leans on the **"Gossip Girl in high-society London"** framing.

### Fandom
- Built almost entirely on **BookTok**: millions of hashtag views, fan-casting, and fierce debate over the central romance fueled the series' climb from self-published to major-publisher juggernaut.
