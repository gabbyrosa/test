> This scores places for two charts at once. It overlays both astrocartography maps, finds where you both have supportive angularity, then casts both relocated charts at each candidate city and scores them on angular planets, the condition of the relocated Ascendant and MC rulers, benefic versus difficult angularity, house emphasis, and whether the city supports the relationship for both of you. Where a couple lives is a real decision with real inputs (work, family, money, and the fact that he loves Pittsburgh), and this is one layer on top of those.

## 1. Method

This follows the protocol you laid out, with one addition: every city is scored **twice**, once per person, and both scores are shown next to the joint score. That way a place that is a 9 for one of you and a 4 for the other gets flagged as **imbalanced** instead of hiding inside a decent-looking average. The naive version of this ("your Venus line crosses his Jupiter line here") can point at a city that is quietly rough for one person on the other three angles. Scoring both full relocated charts catches that.

Scoring, per person per city:
- **Angular planets** within about 6° of the relocated ASC, DESC, MC, or IC. Benefics (Venus, Jupiter) and the Sun score positive, weighted by exactness; Saturn, Mars, and Pluto score negative; Neptune and Uranus score mildly negative.
- A **relationship layer**: Venus, Jupiter, or the ruler of the relocated 7th house going angular for each person, plus whether both of your benefic lines actually converge at that city.
- Joint score = Gabby score + bfs score + relationship bonus, with the balance gap shown.

These are ease-and-support scores. They measure how much a place amplifies the easy parts of a chart. They do **not** measure love or belonging, which is exactly why Pittsburgh needs its own honest section.

## 11. Caveats

- **Both birth times matter a lot here.** Relocated angles and ACG lines move roughly 1° (about 100 km, and one house-cusp degree) for every 4 minutes of birth time. His 12:01 AM and your 3:35 PM are both precise as given; if either turns out to be rounded, the angular placements can shift and a close call (Venus at 0.3° on an angle) could move. Worth confirming both certificate times before acting on a fine distinction.
- **Ease is not everything.** The scores reward benefic angularity. They do not measure meaning, growth, or love, which is why Pittsburgh needed its own section and why a "harder" city can still be the right one.
- **This is a lens, not a plan.** Jobs, family, immigration, cost, language, and community decide where a couple can actually thrive. Use this to choose among places that already work in real life, or to understand why a place feels the way it does.
- **Symbolic throughout.** Astrology here is a language for reflection, offered as such.
