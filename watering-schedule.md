# Garden Box Watering Schedule

Raised beds dry out faster than in-ground gardens. Check soil moisture daily by
sticking your finger 1-2 inches into the soil. Water when it feels dry at that
depth. Best time to water: early morning (6-8 AM). Evening is second best.

## Plant-by-Plant Guide

| Plant     | Water Needs | Frequency (Summer)       | Depth  | Notes                                                    |
|-----------|-------------|--------------------------|--------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Tomatoes  | High        | Every 1-2 days           | Deep   | Water at the base, never on leaves. Consistent moisture prevents blossom end rot. |
| Zucchini  | High        | Every 1-2 days           | Deep   | Needs 1-2 inches per week. Mulch heavily to retain moisture. |
| Peppers   | Moderate    | Every 2-3 days           | Deep   | Let soil dry slightly between waterings. Overwatering causes leaf drop. |
| Spinach   | Moderate    | Every 2-3 days           | Medium | Keep soil consistently moist but not soggy. Bolts faster if stressed. |
| Scallions | Moderate    | Every 2-3 days           | Medium | Shallow roots, so keep top few inches moist.             |
| Basil     | Moderate    | Every 1-2 days           | Medium | Loves moisture but hates soggy roots. Well-drained soil is key. |
| Parsley   | Moderate    | Every 2-3 days           | Medium | Even moisture. Tolerates brief dry spells better than basil. |
| Marigolds | Low-Mod     | Every 3-4 days           | Light  | Drought tolerant once established. Overwatering causes root rot. |
| Thyme     | Low         | Every 4-7 days           | Light  | Prefers to dry out between waterings. Mediterranean plant, likes it lean. |

## Weekly Schedule Template

| Day       | What to Water                                         |
|-----------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Monday    | Everything (deep watering day)                        |
| Tuesday   | Tomatoes, zucchini, basil                             |
| Wednesday | Tomatoes, zucchini, spinach, scallions, peppers, basil, parsley |
| Thursday  | Tomatoes, zucchini, basil                             |
| Friday    | Everything (deep watering day)                        |
| Saturday  | Tomatoes, zucchini, basil                             |
| Sunday    | Tomatoes, zucchini, spinach, scallions, peppers, basil, parsley |

Adjust based on weather: skip a day after rain, water more during heat waves (95F+).

## Hot Weather Adjustments (90F+)

- Water all plants daily
- Water tomatoes and zucchini twice a day if wilting
- Add 2-3 inches of mulch (straw or wood chips) to retain moisture
- Consider shade cloth for spinach (bolts quickly in heat)

## Signs of Overwatering vs. Underwatering

**Underwatered:** wilting (especially in afternoon), dry/crispy leaf edges,
slow growth, soil pulls away from edges of the bed

**Overwatered:** wilting despite wet soil, yellowing lower leaves, mushy stems,
fungus gnats, mold on soil surface

## Supplies for Watering

- Watering can or hose with a gentle shower nozzle
- Soaker hose or drip irrigation kit (optional but saves time)
- Mulch (straw, wood chips, or shredded leaves)
- Moisture meter (optional, takes the guesswork out)
