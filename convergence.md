# Four-method convergence audit, configuration level only

## The shared substrate

All four methods are functions of the same two inputs: this natal chart, and
a location's (lat, lon) which fixes LST and hence the angles. Any two methods
that key on the same angle are not independent witnesses. They are one fact
in two vocabularies.

## Pseudo-convergence: identical loci, different names

  Lee "Venus leaves the 10th for the 9th"   109.120W
  Woods "Venus MC line"                     109.132W     0.012 deg apart

  Lee "Sun leaves the 10th for the 9th"     114.016W
  Woods "Sun MC line"                       114.016W     identical

  Lee "Jupiter enters the 4th"               82.976W
  Woods "Jupiter IC line"                    82.975W     0.001 deg apart

In Placidus the 10th cusp IS the MC and the 4th cusp IS the IC, so a planet
changes 9th/10th exactly at its MC line, by definition. Lee calls
astrocartography "unreliable, misleading, and even dangerous" and then
reproduces three of its lines to three decimals. His objection is to its
INCOMPLETENESS, not its geometry.

The 100.225W boundary is shared by all four and is ONE event: ASC = 0 Libra.
Brennan calls it a whole-sign shift, Siregar-tropical an ASC-ruler change,
Lee a simultaneous flip of all twelve Placidus cusp signs. It is
latitude-invariant because 0 Libra is the autumnal equinox. That is celestial
mechanics, not four independent confirmations.

## Near-miss that reads as convergence but is not

  Siregar sidereal Leo|Virgo boundary       107.764W   (ASC = 23.8 Virgo)
  Venus MC line                             109.132W   (MC = Venus)

1.37 deg apart, about 75 miles. Causally unrelated: change the ayanamsa and
one moves while the other does not. On a map they look like one zone.

## Convention dependence

Lee (Placidus + modern) vs Hellenistic (whole sign + traditional), key-house
ruler disagreement across the US: 23% of all (location, house) pairs.

  h1   0%     h4   2%     h9   5%     h10  2%
  h11 13%     h7  43%     h2  57%     h5  63%

Placidus and whole sign agree on key houses almost everywhere, so this is
almost entirely the traditional/modern ruler split, not the house system.
Identity, home, travel and career rulers are convention-robust. Money,
children and partnership are not.
