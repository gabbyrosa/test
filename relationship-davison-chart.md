## What these two charts are

- **Composite chart (midpoint method):** every planet is the midpoint of your two planets, and the angles are the midpoints of your two angles. It is a symbolic construct, not a real moment in time, and it describes the *inner nature* of the bond. It cannot really be relocated.
- **Davison chart (time-space midpoint):** a **real chart** cast for the exact midpoint in time between your two births, at the geographic midpoint of your two birthplaces. Because it is a real moment, it has real planetary positions and it *can* be relocated, which is what makes it the right tool for "where does the relationship thrive."

Your Davison midpoint lands on **December 22, 1995, near Pittsburgh** (both of you were born there, so the midpoint place simply is Pittsburgh). December 22 is the winter solstice, which is why the Davison Sun sits at 0° Capricorn, a cardinal world-point.

## Caveats

- **Composite and Davison are interpretive constructs.** The composite is not a real moment at all; the Davison is a real chart but for a midpoint that neither of you experienced. They are established relationship techniques, used here as symbolic lenses, not literal readings.
- **Relocation still depends on both birth times.** The Davison time is the average of your two, so an error in either birth time shifts it and its angles. Both of yours are precise as given; confirm the certificates before acting on a tight call.
- **Symbolic throughout,** and one layer on top of the real-life inputs that actually decide where a couple lives.
