## Start here (my top 3 for you)

1. **Binding 13** (Boys of Tommen #1) by **Chloe Walsh** - the closest thing to the Magnolia Parks feeling: a slow-burn, found-family, emotionally shredding series set in an elite Irish school. Six books to binge. If you loved living inside the MPU, this is your next universe.
2. **Daisy Jones & The Six** by **Taylor Jenkins Reid** - a 1970s rock band told like a documentary. Fame, a doomed almost-romance, and the exact glamour-and-devastation of *Time of Your Life*.
3. **Tell Me Lies** by **Carola Lovering** - the toxic, obsessive one. A college relationship you cannot look away from, told from both sides. Straight down the *My Husband* / *Sunburn* lane.

## 1. Closest to the Magnolia Parks Universe
*Messy, angsty, morally grey, love triangles, big interconnected series to binge.*

- **Boys of Tommen series** by **Chloe Walsh** - reading order: *Binding 13 -> Keeping 13 -> Saving 6 -> Redeeming 6 -> Taming 7 -> Releasing 10*. Emotional devastation, slow burn, trauma, found family. The fandom overlap with MPU readers is enormous.
- **The Summer We Fell** (The Devil series) by **Elizabeth O'Roark** - love triangle, forbidden, angst, second chances. Very MPU-adjacent emotional stakes.
- **The Ravenhood series** (start with **Flock**) by **Kate Stewart** - secret society, morally grey, slow burn, a love triangle that wrecks people. Beloved for how hard it hits.
- **Twisted series** (start with **Twisted Love**) by **Ana Huang** - possessive, rich, morally grey heroes with banter. Lighter and hotter than MPU but the same "rich people, big feelings" energy, and it's a four-book interconnected set.
- **Corrupt** (Devil's Night) by **Penelope Douglas** - dark, rich boys behaving very badly, revenge, enemies to lovers. This is the "rich people behaving badly" trope at full volume.

## 2. Fame, rock stars, and glamour
*For the Time of Your Life and Into the Blue side of you.*

- **The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo** by **Taylor Jenkins Reid** - old Hollywood, a ruthless, glamorous, morally grey icon, and a love story that devastates. If you've somehow missed it, move it up.
- **Songs in Ursa Major** by **Emma Brodie** - since you loved her *Into the Blue*, this is her earlier one: a 1970s music-scene romance in the *Daisy Jones* vein.
- **The Idea of You** by **Robinne Lee** - an older woman and a boy-band star. Glamorous, swoony, and quietly devastating.
- **Funny You Should Ask** by **Elissa Sussman** - a journalist and the movie star she interviewed, told across two timelines. Charming and aching.
- **Malibu Rising** by **Taylor Jenkins Reid** - one wild night, a famous surfing family, secrets detonating. Glossy, dramatic, bingeable.

## 3. Obsessive and dark love
*For the My Husband and Sunburn streak: intense, unsettling, literary.*

- **Cleopatra and Frankenstein** by **Coco Mellors** - a glamorous, impulsive New York marriage that is equal parts intoxicating and toxic. Beautiful and messy. (Her follow-up **Blue Sisters** is also excellent.)
- **You** by **Caroline Kepnes** - the obsessive narrator to end all obsessive narrators. Pairs perfectly with the unsettling POV of *My Husband*.
- **Normal People** by **Sally Rooney** - on-again, off-again yearning, class and power, quietly wrecking. The literary cousin of your angst.
- **The Paper Palace** by **Miranda Cowley Heller** - a lifelong love triangle and one impossible decision, over a single charged day. Literary and devastating.
- **One Day** by **David Nicholls** - two people, the same date across twenty years. Peak yearning. Bring tissues.

## 4. Rich people behaving badly (glossy drama)
*Pure glamour, secrets, and bad decisions.*

- **The Guest** by **Emma Cline** - a young woman drifting through a Hamptons summer she doesn't belong in. Tense, glamorous, unnerving.
- **Malibu Rising** by **Taylor Jenkins Reid** - (also in the fame section) works here too for the family-money-and-scandal appeal.
- **Gossip Girl** by **Cecily von Ziegesar** - if you want the frothy, unserious, Upper-East-Side original that basically invented this whole vibe.
