## Start here (my top 3 for you)

1. **Binding 13** (Boys of Tommen #1) by **Chloe Walsh** - the perfect bridge between your Jessa Hastings and Elle Kennedy shelves: an angsty, slow-burn, found-family sports saga set in an elite Irish school. Six books to disappear into.
2. **Sorrow and Bliss** by **Meg Mason** - for your Dolly Alderton and Caroline O'Donoghue side. Brutally funny, quietly devastating, about a marriage and a mind coming apart. Award-winning and unforgettable.
3. **Cleopatra and Frankenstein** by **Coco Mellors** - the crossover pick: a glamorous, impulsive New York marriage that is equal parts intoxicating and toxic. Messy romance energy with literary heft.

## 1. Messy, glamorous, angsty love
*Closest to the Jessa Hastings feeling: love triangles, morally grey, big series to binge.*

- **Boys of Tommen series** by **Chloe Walsh** - reading order: *Binding 13 -> Keeping 13 -> Saving 6 -> Redeeming 6 -> Taming 7 -> Releasing 10*. Emotional devastation, slow burn, trauma, found family. The MPU reader crossover is enormous.
- **The Summer We Fell** (The Devil #1) by **Elizabeth O'Roark** - love triangle, forbidden, second chances, angst turned all the way up.
- **Twisted Love** by **Ana Huang** - possessive, rich, morally grey hero with real banter. Hotter and lighter than MPU, four interconnected books, easy to binge.

## 2. Sports and campus romance
*You are clearly caught up on Elle Kennedy, Liz Tomforde, and Ali Hazelwood, so here are fresh series in that exact lane.*

- **Icebreaker** (Maple Hills #1) by **Hannah Grace** - figure skater meets hockey captain, forced proximity, spicy and swoony. The definitive BookTok hockey romance.
- **Behind the Net** (Vancouver Storm #1) by **Stephanie Archer** - grumpy goalie, sunshine heroine, slow burn, pro hockey, lots of spice. Reads like prime Elle Kennedy.
- **Deep End** by **Ali Hazelwood** - since you read her *Problematic Summer Romance*, this is her steamiest standalone: elite divers, a dom/sub dynamic, and her signature banter.

## 3. Witty, warm romance
*For the Emily Henry, Christina Lauren, and Carley Fortune part of you.*

- **Beach Read** by **Emily Henry** - since you read *Funny Story*, work backward through her: this is the one that started it, a burned-out romance writer and a literary snob who swap genres for a summer.
- **Just for the Summer** by **Abby Jimenez** - two people who are both "good luck charms" for their exes decide to date each other. Funny, emotional, and a genuine tearjerker.
- **It Happened One Summer** by **Tessa Bailey** - a glam LA it-girl exiled to a small fishing town falls for a broody sea captain. *Schitt's Creek* meets a spicy rom-com.
- *Keep going with authors you love:* Emily Henry's **Book Lovers** and **Happy Place**, and Carley Fortune's **Every Summer After**.

## 4. Fame and glamour
*For the Time of Your Life and Into the Blue side of you.*

- **Daisy Jones & The Six** by **Taylor Jenkins Reid** *(if you haven't already)* - a 1970s rock band told like a documentary. The exact glamour-and-wreckage of *Time of Your Life*.
- **The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo** by **Taylor Jenkins Reid** *(if you haven't already)* - old Hollywood, a ruthless morally grey icon, and a love story that devastates.
- **The Idea of You** by **Robinne Lee** - an older woman and a boy-band star. Glamorous, swoony, quietly devastating.
- **Songs in Ursa Major** by **Emma Brodie** - since you loved her *Into the Blue*, here is her earlier one: a 1970s music-scene romance in the *Daisy Jones* vein.

## 5. Smart, sad, funny literary fiction
*For your Dolly Alderton, Caroline O'Donoghue, and Jenny Jackson shelf.*

- **Sorrow and Bliss** by **Meg Mason** - funny and devastating in the same breath. If you loved *Good Material*, start here.
- **Really Good, Actually** by **Monica Heisey** - a very online, very funny novel about being a "surprisingly young divorcee." Peak millennial voice.
- **Exciting Times** by **Naoise Dolan** - a sharp Irish love triangle between a teacher, a banker, and a lawyer in Hong Kong. Constantly compared to Sally Rooney.
- **Normal People** by **Sally Rooney** - on-again, off-again yearning shot through with class and power. Quietly wrecking.
- *More from authors already on your shelf:* Dolly Alderton's **Ghosts**, Caroline O'Donoghue's **Promising Young Women**, and Coco Mellors' **Blue Sisters**.

## 6. Obsessive and unsettling love
*For the My Husband and Sunburn streak, and your domestic-suspense habit.*

- **Tell Me Lies** by **Carola Lovering** - a toxic, obsessive college relationship you cannot look away from, told from both sides.
- **You** by **Caroline Kepnes** - the obsessive narrator to end all obsessive narrators. The bridge between your *My Husband* taste and your thriller shelf.
- **The Paper Palace** by **Miranda Cowley Heller** - a lifelong love triangle and one impossible decision, over a single charged day. Literary and devastating.
- **One Day** by **David Nicholls** - two people, the same date across twenty years. Peak yearning. Bring tissues.
